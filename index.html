Short Bio

 Hi, My name is Min Zhou, a PhD Student at Cardiff University!
From 2024.10 -Now, I start my research study on "Narrative game" "Exergame " focus on Older adults to improve their mental health and physical activity, supervised by Dr. Soumya Barathi. And Dr Katarzyna (Kathy) Stawarz And Prof Jose Camacho-Collados
Previously, I earned my M.A. in Industrial Design from the Beijing Institute of Technology. My research experience includes internships at the Chinese Academy of Sciences and the International Design Institute of Zhejiang University. My work aims to leverage immersive technology to support older adults.
Research Interests: Exergames in VR/AR; Digital Games; Narrative Games; Aging and Mental Health.
