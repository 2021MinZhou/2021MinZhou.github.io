Short Bio

 Hi, My name is Min Zhou , the Phd student in Cardiff University!
From 2024.10 -Now, I start my research study on "Narrative game" "Exergame " focus on Older adults to improve their mental health and physical activity, supervised by Dr. Soumya Barathi. And Dr Katarzyna (Kathy) Stawarz. And Prof JOSE CAMACHO COLLADOS.
Previously, I earned my M.A. in Industrial Design from the Beijing Institute of Technology. My research experience includes internships at the Chinese Academy of Sciences (investigating emotional challenges in virtual spaces) and the International Design Institute of Zhejiang University (working on conversational agents). My work aims to leverage immersive technology to support vulnerable populations, including older adults and children with autism.
Currently, I focus on : Exergames in VR/AR; Digital game; Narrative Game; Older adults; Mental Health; Children game .
